Before you apply

A few habits that save applications from getting rejected.

Watch the last date

Apply a few days early. Portals slow down near the deadline.

Read the official notification

Every listing links to the source. Check eligibility and documents there before you start.

Keep documents ready

CV, ID proof, marksheets and a short statement of purpose make most applications quick.
